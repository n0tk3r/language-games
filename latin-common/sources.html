Where the words and charts come from

The Latin games practice two things from each chapter of the class textbook: its vocabulary and the charts of its model words. This page says where each comes from, so you know what your child is drilling.

The book

Wheelock’s Latin, 7th edition, by Frederic M. Wheelock, revised by Richard A. LaFleur (ISBN 978-0061997211). This is the edition on Hillsdale College’s K-12 book list (K-12 Sample Book List, and Resources for Latin Students in 6th-12th). The games follow its chapters, its dictionary entries and its order of the cases: nominative, genitive, dative, accusative, ablative, vocative.

Grades and months

Which chapters belong to which grade, and the month each chapter is taught, come from Hillsdale College’s K-12 curriculum documents: the Curriculum Overview and the “Year at a Glance” pages of the Program Guide.

  • 6th grade, Latin IA: chapters 1 to 8
  • 7th grade, Latin IB: chapters 9 to 17 (coming to the games later)
  • 8th grade, Latin II: chapters 18 to 27
  • 9th grade, Latin III: chapters 28 to 40 (coming to the games later)

The words

The word lists were taken from the University of Victoria’s “Wheelock Latin Exercises” flashcards (hcmc.uvic.ca/project/latin/wheelock) and cross-checked with the lists at warmenhoven.org. An online list can differ from the printed book by a word or a meaning, so check the printed chapter list before a graded test.

The charts

The charts of endings follow the book’s layout. Their forms were checked against the official Wheelock’s Latin GrammarQuick card (wheelockslatin.com) and the University of Victoria’s 7th-edition pages.

Everything else

The English derivatives (the “think of” hints) and the games’ own texts were written for the games and reviewed.

Names and privacy

A player’s name, grade, chapter and progress are kept only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the games load nothing from other sites.

These games are not made or endorsed by Hillsdale College or by the publishers of Wheelock’s Latin.

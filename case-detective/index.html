Ask every noun a question

NominativeWho does it? The subject.
GenitiveWhose is it? Possession, “of”.
DativeWho receives it? “to / for”.
AccusativeWhat gets the action? Direct object.
Ablative“by / with / from / in”.
VocativeTalking to someone. “Ō puella!”

Cracking the sneaky -ae

  1. Check the verb. Ends in -nt (dant, amant)? Then a plural subject is doing it: nominative plural.
  2. Is it a giving verb (dat, dant)? Somebody must receive: dative.
  3. All those jobs taken? Then -ae tells you whose: genitive.
